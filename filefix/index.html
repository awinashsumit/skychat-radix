Nothing leaves your device. Every image is decoded, resized and re-encoded by your own browser — there is no server and no upload. Re-encoding also drops EXIF metadata, so GPS coordinates and camera details are stripped on the way out.

HEIC files from an iPhone only decode in Safari; elsewhere, convert them first. PDFs and video are out of scope — a browser cannot re-encode those without a heavy library.
